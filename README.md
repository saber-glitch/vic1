A# 🦇 CRIMSON NIGHT: VAMPIRE SURVIVORS

A fast-paced, gothic roguelite auto-shooter horde survival game built with **Python & Pygame**, inspired by **Vampire Survivors**.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

### Option 1: Double-Click Launcher (Windows)
Double-click **`run_game.bat`** in this folder to launch the game instantly.

### Option 2: Command Line
```powershell
python main.py
```
*(Or use the user-installed Python at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Python\Python312\python.exe main.py`)*

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Controls |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aim Weapons & Face Hunter** | **Mouse Cursor** — all directional weapons and character facing track your mouse! |
| **Move Hunter** | `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys` (8-directional movement) |
| **Attacking** | **Automatic towards Cursor** — weapons fire in the direction of your mouse |
| **Select Level-Up Upgrade** | Click with **Mouse** or press `1`, `2`, `3` or `Up`/`Down` + `Enter` |
| **Pause / Resume** | `ESC` |
| **Toggle Audio Mute** | `M` |
| **Restart (on Game Over)** | `R` or `Space` |

## ⚔️ Weapons & Abilities

You can equip up to **6 weapons** simultaneously:

1. **Holy Whip**: Slashes in the exact 360° direction of your mouse cursor! Upgrades widen the arc, increase damage, and add reverse slashes behind you.
2. **Magic Wand**: Fires rapid arcane projectiles aimed directly at your mouse cursor. Upgrades add extra missiles, pierce, and reduce cooldown.
3. **Garlic Aura**: Generates a continuous pulsating holy protective circle around you that damages and pushes back swarming enemies.
4. **Holy Water**: Lobs blessed flasks directly towards your mouse cursor position, creating lingering pools of blue holy fire that incinerate monsters.
5. **King's Bible**: Holy scriptures that continuously orbit around you in a protective spinning wall of damage and knockback.
6. **Lightning Ring**: Calls down divine thunderbolts from the heavens upon foes closest to your mouse cursor, inflicting catastrophic area damage.

---

## 🛡️ Passive Upgrades

Augment your hunter with up to **6 passive items**:

- **Spinach**: +10% Damage per level to all weapons.
- **Hollow Heart**: +20% Max HP and instantly restores 20 HP.
- **Wings**: +12% Movement Speed per level.
- **Crown**: +15% Experience earned per level.
- **Attractorb**: +35% Pickup Magnet Radius per level.
- **Armor Plate**: Decreases incoming damage by 1 per hit.
- **Pummarola**: Regenerates +0.4 HP per second.
- **Empty Tome**: Reduces all weapon cooldowns by -8% per level.

## 💀 Enemies & Bosses

- 🦇 **Bats**: Fast, low-health swarmers that rush in tight packs.
- 💀 **Skeletons**: Steady frontline marchers with glowing cyan eye sockets.
- 🧟 **Zombies**: Heavy, lumbering undead with high health pools.
- 👻 **Wraiths**: Ethereal ghosts that weave back and forth unpredictably.
- 🪨 **Gargoyles**: Armored stone demons with high damage resistance.
- 👑 **Vampire Lord (Boss)**: Colossal boss appearing at 2:00, 5:00, and milestone intervals. Defeating him drops a **Treasure Chest**!

## 💎 Pickups & Collectibles

- **XP Gems**:
  - 🔷 **Blue Gem**: 1 XP
  - 🟢 **Green Gem**: 5 XP
  - 🔴 **Red Gem**: 25 XP
  - 🟡 **Gold Gem**: 100 XP
- 🍗 **Roast Meat**: Heals 30-35 HP.
- 🧲 **Vacuum Magnet**: Sucks every single XP gem on the entire map straight to you!
- ✝️ **Rosary**: Unleashes a radiant holy blast that obliterates all standard monsters on screen!
- 🎁 **Treasure Chest**: Dropped by bosses; awards instant free upgrades!

## 🎨 Audio & Visual Polish

- **100% Self-Contained Procedural Sound Synthesizer**: All 16-bit retro sound effects (whip whooshes, gem pings, lightning strikes, fanfare, level-up jingles, explosions) are generated dynamically in memory via mathematical PCM synthesis.
- **Juicy Combat Effects**: Floating damage numbers, critical strike notifications, healing popups, screen shake trauma, enemy flash-on-hit, and particle blood bursts.
- **Infinite Gothic World**: Endless scrolling cobblestone and graveyard terrain with tombstone props and dynamic camera tracking.
